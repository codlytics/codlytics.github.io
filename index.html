Codlytics

One clear page for the money side of your Android apps.

What it does

Codlytics is a reporting dashboard for app owners and the teams that advertise their apps. It brings an app's revenue, advertising spend, return on ad spend (ROAS), installs, ratings, crashes and in-app purchases into one place, and tells you in plain words what to fix next.

What it connects to

  • Google Analytics and BigQuery for users, campaign cost and the Analytics export.
  • AdMob for ad revenue.
  • Google Play Console for ratings, reviews, crash and ANR rates and purchase reports.

Access is read-only. Codlytics cannot change, post, delete or spend anything in your Google accounts. You choose what to connect, and you can remove access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your data

We keep daily totals needed for the dashboard (for example users, revenue and cost per day and country). We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with other customers. Read the full Privacy Policy.
